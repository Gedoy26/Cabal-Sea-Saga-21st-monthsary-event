READ BEFORE PARTICIPATING

Event Guidelines

01 — Event Participation

  • Players must enter their correct IGN (In-Game Name).
  • Make sure your IGN is spelled correctly.
  • Each IGN should only have one entry in the Event Tracker.
  • Duplicate entries may be removed or combined.

02 — Referral System

  • Only valid and verifiable referrals will be counted.
  • Duplicate, fake, or invalid referrals will not be included.
  • Referral counts may be checked and verified by event staff.

03 — Referral Deadline

  • All referrals must be submitted on or before September 7, 2026.
  • Referrals submitted after the deadline will not be counted.

04 — Rankings

  • Rankings are determined by the total number of valid referrals.
  • More valid referrals result in a higher rank.
  • In case of a tie, event staff may use the time and date the referral count was reached as a tie-breaker.

05 — Updating & Disputes

  • The tracker may not update immediately after every submission.
  • Event staff may review and update entries periodically.
  • Contact event staff if you believe your referral count is incorrect.
  • The decision of the event staff regarding disputed or invalid referrals is final.

06 — Fair Play

  • Manipulating the tracker, submitting fake referrals, using duplicate accounts, or abusing the referral system is strictly prohibited.
  • Violators will be punished accordingly.
  • Penalties may include removal of invalid entries, loss of rewards, disqualification, or other penalties deemed appropriate by event staff.
