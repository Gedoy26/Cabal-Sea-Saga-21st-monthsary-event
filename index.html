EVENT GUIDELINES

01

Event Duration & Deadline

The CABAL SEA SAGA Referral Event will run until September 7, 2026. All referrals must be completed and submitted before the official deadline.

Any referral submitted after the deadline may not be counted. Participants are responsible for making sure their referrals are submitted within the allotted event period.

02

Referral Requirements

Participants must follow the official referral requirements provided by the CABAL SEA SAGA team. The complete referral requirements, instructions, and verification process will be sent directly through DMs.

Participants must provide the required information accurately and completely. Failure to provide the required information may result in the referral being considered invalid.

03

Valid Referrals

Only referrals that satisfy all event requirements and successfully pass verification will be counted toward a participant's total referral count.

A referral may be rejected or removed if it is found to be invalid, duplicated, falsely submitted, manipulated, or otherwise fails to meet the official referral requirements.

The event organizers reserve the right to verify referral submissions and determine whether a referral is valid.

04

Referral Count & Standings

The leaderboard will display the current standings of participating players based on their total number of valid referrals.

Rankings may change throughout the event as new valid referrals are submitted and verified. The standings displayed on the website represent the current recorded referral counts and may not represent the final result until the event has officially ended.

Participants are encouraged to regularly check the leaderboard and report any suspected discrepancies to the event organizers.

05

Ranking & Rewards

Participants will be ranked according to their total number of valid referrals at the end of the event. Certain rankings will receive corresponding rewards.

The reward assigned to each qualifying rank will be announced and displayed through the official event channels and leaderboard.

Being temporarily placed within a reward-ranking position during the event does not guarantee a reward. Final rankings will be determined after the referral period has ended and the submitted referrals have undergone final verification.

06

Fair Play

All participants are expected to participate fairly and follow the rules and instructions provided by the CABAL SEA SAGA team.

Participants must not attempt to manipulate the referral system, falsify referral information, create artificial referrals, abuse loopholes, or use any method intended to gain an unfair advantage over other participants.

Any activity that compromises the fairness or integrity of the event may be investigated by the event organizers.

07

Violations & Punishment

Any participant found violating the event rules will be punished accordingly.

Depending on the severity of the violation, penalties may include removal of invalid referrals, adjustment of referral counts, removal from the leaderboard, disqualification from the event, forfeiture of rewards, or other appropriate disciplinary action.

The event organizers reserve the right to review suspicious activity and determine the appropriate action for violations.

08

Final Decision

The CABAL SEA SAGA event organizers reserve the right to verify referral submissions, correct referral counts, remove invalid entries, and make adjustments to the leaderboard when necessary.

Decisions made by the event organizers regarding the validity of referrals, violations, rankings, and rewards shall be considered final.

The organizers also reserve the right to clarify, update, or modify event rules when necessary to maintain the fairness and integrity of the event.
